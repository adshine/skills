# Technical-to-Business Translation Dictionary

When preparing Executive Summaries, map technical findings directly to business outcomes:

| Technical Symptom | Plain-English Explanation | Executive & Financial Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LCP > 4.0s (Largest Contentful Paint)** | The main content/product image takes over 4 seconds to appear on mobile screens. | **High Bounce Rate & Lost Sales:** Every 1-second delay reduces conversion rates by 7-10%. Paid traffic bounces before seeing the offer. |
| **INP > 500ms (Interaction to Next Paint)** | Buttons and menus feel laggy or freeze for half a second after being clicked. | **Cart Abandonment:** Frustrated users believe the site is unresponsive or broken and leave. |
| **CLS > 0.25 (Cumulative Layout Shift)** | Content moves around unexpectedly while the page is loading. | **Accidental Clicks & User Frustration:** Users accidentally click wrong buttons or ads while attempting to navigate. |
| **Missing Content-Security-Policy (CSP)** | The website lacks browser guardrails against unauthorized injected code. | **Data Theft & Brand Damage:** Customer payment information and session cookies can be stolen via third-party scripts. |
| **WCAG 2.1 AA Contrast / Screen Reader Failure** | Visually impaired or motor-impaired users cannot read text or submit forms. | **Legal Liability & Lost Market Share:** 15%+ of users are shut out; creates direct exposure to ADA Title III lawsuits and fines. |
| **Missing Canonical Tags / 404 Spikes** | Search engines find duplicate URLs and broken links across the website. | **Lost Organic Traffic & Higher Ad Spend:** Search ranking drops, forcing increased paid acquisition spend to compensate. |
| **Unconsented Analytics / Tracking Pixels** | Marketing cookies load before visitors click "Accept" on the privacy banner. | **Regulatory Fines:** Direct non-compliance penalties under GDPR (up to 4% global annual turnover) and 20+ US state privacy laws, several with no grace period to fix violations. |
| **GPC Opt-Out Signal Ignored** | Visitors who turned on a browser privacy setting are still being tracked and their data shared. | **State Privacy Law Violations:** California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and others require honoring this signal; regulators have already fined companies for ignoring it. |
| **AI Crawlers Blocked / Content Not Answer-Ready** | ChatGPT, Google's AI results, and other AI assistants cannot read or cite the website. | **Invisible in AI Search:** A growing share of buyers ask AI tools before visiting websites; competitors get recommended instead. |
| **Missing Structured Data (Schema)** | The site doesn't give search engines machine-readable labels for products, FAQs, and business info. | **Fewer Rich Results:** Competitors get star ratings, prices, and FAQ dropdowns in search results; this site gets a plain blue link. |
| **Third-Party Scripts Without Integrity Checks** | Outside code on payment pages could be swapped for malicious code without anyone noticing. | **Card-Skimming Exposure:** This is how Magecart attacks steal customer card data; leads to breach costs, fines, and brand damage. |
| **No Accessibility Statement (EU Sales)** | The site is missing a legally required public statement about its accessibility. | **EAA Fines Per Violation:** Enforceable across the EU since June 2025 for sites selling to EU consumers. |
