## When to use

- For frequently asked questions and help content where people look for one answer.
- To shorten long pages on small screens, like product details or terms.
- To group optional settings people rarely change.

## When not to use

- **For content most people need.** Show it. Hiding it adds a click for everyone.
- **To switch between views of the same thing.** Use [tabs](/components/tabs).
- **For a single short section.** A link like "Show details" is lighter.
- **For required form fields.** People may miss them inside a closed section.

## Single or multiple

With `type="single"` only one section is open at a time; add `collapsible` so people can close it again. With `type="multiple"` people can open several at once, which suits comparing answers.

## Content

- Headings are specific: a question, or a noun phrase like "Shipping and delivery".
- Sentence case, no full stop unless the heading is a question.
- Keep each panel short. If a panel needs headings of its own, it should be a page.
- Order sections by how often people need them.

`AccordionTrigger` renders the heading button with a chevron that turns when open. `AccordionContent` animates open and closed; its `className` applies to the inner padding wrapper.

## Heading level

Each trigger sits inside a Radix `Header`, which renders an `h3`. Place accordions under an `h2` section heading so the page outline stays in order.

## Styling

Sections are split with `border-b` from the `--border` role. The chevron uses `--muted-foreground`, and the open and close motion uses the `accordion-down` and `accordion-up` animations.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Moves to the next heading or focusable element inside an open panel |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | Opens or closes the focused section |
| <kbd>Arrow down</kbd> / <kbd>Arrow up</kbd> | Moves to the next or previous heading |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | Moves to the first or last heading |

## Focus management

- Opening a section keeps focus on its heading. Content is next in the <kbd>Tab</kbd> order.
- Closed panels are removed from the page, so nothing hidden can receive focus.

## Checklist

- Each trigger is a button inside a heading, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` set.
- Each panel has `role="region"` and is labelled by its heading.
- Open state is shown by the chevron direction and the visible panel, not by color alone.
- Focus rings use `--ring` and are visible on every heading.
- The open and close motion is short and only changes height, so content below moves down rather than sideways.
