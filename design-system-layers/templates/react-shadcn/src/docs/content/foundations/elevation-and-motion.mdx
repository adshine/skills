## Borders before shadows

Most surfaces sit flat on the page and are separated by a 1px `border` line. Shadows are reserved for things that float above the page, so a shadow always means "this is on top and will go away".

| Level | Treatment | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Page | `bg-background`, no border | The page itself |
| 1. Surface | `border` with `bg-card`, optional `shadow-xs` or `shadow-sm` | Cards, inputs, buttons, tables |
| 2. Floating | `border` with `bg-popover` and `shadow-md` | Menus, selects, popovers, tooltips |
| 3. Modal | `border` with `shadow-lg`, over a dimmed backdrop | Dialogs and sheets |

## Overlays

- **One modal at a time.** Do not open a dialog from a dialog. Replace the content or use a step inside the same dialog.
- **Dim the page behind a modal** with the dialog's built-in backdrop. It tells people the page is paused.
- **Popovers and menus do not dim the page.** They close on <kbd>Esc</kbd>, on outside click and when focus leaves them.
- **Toasts float above everything** but never block an action. Do not put the only copy of important information in a toast.
- Let the components manage stacking order. Do not add `z-` classes by hand to win a stacking fight; that usually means an overlay is inside the wrong container.

## Motion principles

- **Motion explains change.** Use it to show where something came from or went, not to decorate.
- **Fast and quiet.** Most UI motion finishes in under 250ms. People should never wait for an animation.
- **Interruptible.** If someone acts mid-animation, respond right away.

## Durations

| Duration | Class | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 to 150ms | `duration-100`, `duration-150` | Hover, press and color changes, checkbox ticks |
| 200ms | `duration-200` | Menus, popovers, tooltips, accordions, dialogs opening |
| 300ms | `duration-300` | Sheets and larger panels sliding in |
| Over 400ms | Avoid | Only for one-off moments like a first-run welcome |

Exits are slightly faster than entrances. Things leave about 25% quicker than they arrived, so the next view is not held up.

## Easing

| Easing | Class | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ease out | `ease-out` | Things entering or opening. Starts fast, settles gently. |
| Ease in | `ease-in` | Things leaving. Use only on exits. |
| Ease in and out | `ease-in-out` | Things moving from one place on screen to another. |
| Linear | `ease-linear` | Progress bars and spinners only. |

Animate only `opacity` and `transform` where possible. Animating size, margins or position causes layout work and stutters on slower phones.

## Reduced motion

Some people get dizzy or sick from movement, and set "Reduce motion" in their system settings. Respect it everywhere:

- Add `motion-reduce:transition-none` or `motion-reduce:animate-none` to anything that moves or scales.
- Keep the state change itself: the panel still opens, it just appears without sliding.
- Opacity fades and color changes are fine to keep, since they do not create a sense of movement.
- Never auto-play motion that lasts more than 5 seconds without a way to pause it.

```tsx
<div className="transition-transform duration-300 ease-out motion-reduce:transition-none">
  ...
</div>
```

Loading spinners may keep spinning under reduced motion because they show that work is happening, but prefer a [skeleton](/patterns/empty-loading-error-states) for content that is loading.
