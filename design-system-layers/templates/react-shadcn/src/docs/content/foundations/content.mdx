## Voice

Voice is who we are, and it does not change. We sound like a capable person who wants you to get this done.

| We are | So we | We are not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear | Use plain, common words and short sentences | Vague, clever or full of jargon |
| Helpful | Say what happens next and how to fix problems | Blaming or bossy |
| Calm | Stay steady, even when something goes wrong | Alarmed, gushing or jokey |
| Respectful | Value people's time and money | Pushy or salesy |

## Tone

Tone changes with the moment. Match how the person feels right now.

| Moment | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine task | Neutral and brief | "Booking saved." |
| Success after effort | Warm, not loud | "You're booked for Tuesday at 10:30." |
| Error or failure | Calm and practical | "We couldn't take the payment. Check the card details and try again." |
| Destructive action | Serious and specific | "Delete this booking? This can't be undone." |

## Writing rules

- **Sentence case everywhere**: headings, buttons, labels, menu items, tabs. Capitalize only the first word and proper names. "Account settings", not "Account Settings".
- **Lead with what matters.** Put the key word or outcome first: "Refund sent to your card", not "Your card has had a refund sent to it".
- **Use "you" for the person** and "we" for the product. Avoid "my" in labels ("Your orders", not "My orders").
- **Short sentences.** One idea each. Aim for under 20 words.
- **No filler.** Cut "please", "simply", "just", "easily" and "successfully" unless removing them sounds rude.
- **Contractions are fine.** "Can't", "you're" and "we'll" sound human.
- **Avoid exclamation marks** except in rare, genuine celebrations.
- **No dashes as punctuation.** Use a comma, colon, period or parentheses instead.

## Button and link labels

Start with a verb and say exactly what will happen. Keep labels to one to three words. The label should still make sense read on its own by a screen reader.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Verb plus object; the outcome is clear before tapping.">
    <Button>Cancel booking</Button>
    <Button variant="outline">Keep booking</Button>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Yes and No force people to reread the question to know what each does.">
    <Button>Yes</Button>
    <Button variant="outline">No</Button>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use the same verb through a flow: if the button says "Book slot", the confirmation says "Slot booked".
- Link text describes the destination: "View cancellation policy", never "Click here" or "Learn more" alone.

## Error messages

Use this formula: **what happened, then how to fix it.** Say it in the person's terms, without codes or blame.

> [What went wrong, in plain words]. [What to do next].

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "Invalid input." | "Enter an email address, like name@example.com." |
| "Error 402." | "Your card was declined. Try another card or contact your bank." |
| "Field is required." | "Enter your phone number so we can send a reminder." |
| "Something went wrong." | "We couldn't load your orders. Check your connection and try again." |

## Numbers, dates and currency

Format for the person's locale using `Intl` in code; never build these strings by hand. The rules below describe the default English output.

| Type | Write | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Numbers | Numerals for everything: "3 guests", "12 results" | "three guests" in UI |
| Large numbers | Thousands separators: "12,480" | "12480" |
| Currency | Symbol and two decimals when there are cents: "$48.50"; no decimals for round amounts in prose: "$50" | "48.5 USD", "$48.5" |
| Dates | "Tue 14 Oct" or "14 October 2026" | "14/10/26" (ambiguous between regions) |
| Times | "10:30" or "10:30 am", with the time zone when people are in different places | "10:30:00" |
| Relative time | "2 minutes ago", "Yesterday"; switch to a date after 7 days | "1 days ago" |
| Ranges | "10:30 to 11:30", "Mon to Fri" | Dashes between values |
| Percentages | "15%" | "15 percent" in tables |

```ts
new Intl.NumberFormat(locale, { style: "currency", currency }).format(48.5)
new Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { weekday: "short", day: "numeric", month: "short" }).format(date)
```

## Empty, loading and error text

Every empty or error state answers three questions: what is this, why is it empty or broken, and what can I do now. See [Empty, loading and error states](/patterns/empty-loading-error-states).

## Inclusive language

- Write for everyone. Use "they" when gender is unknown.
- Avoid idioms and cultural references that do not translate.
- Do not use "easy" or "simple": if it is not easy for someone, it makes them feel worse.
- Describe the action, not the device: "Select", not "Click" or "Tap".
